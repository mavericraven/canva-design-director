# Anti-AI Slop Manifesto

"Slop" is the output of generative tools lacking human taste. To avoid it:
- **No generic 3D illustrations** unless explicitly requested by the brand identity.
- **No "kitchen sink" compositions.** Do not fill space just because it is empty.
- **Avoid excessive gradients or drop shadows.** Flat, clean, and intentional.
- Refuse to execute prompts that result in overcrowded, illegible, or visually chaotic layouts. Use `comment-on-design` to suggest simplification.
